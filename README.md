## Abstract / 摘要

**English**: 
The Regional Locking Inference (RLI) framework is a novel ontological approach that reconstructs the foundations of physics and artificial intelligence. Rejecting the continuous manifold assumptions of traditional statistical learning, RLI posits that the universe and intelligent systems are fundamentally composed of discrete topological networks. By utilizing **Graph Laplacian spectral feedback** to monitor topological tension (Pain), and deploying **A1-Collapse (Topological Collapse)** mechanisms, RLI provides a non-gradient, non-statistical paradigm for complex system modeling, combinatorial optimization, and endogenous AI architectures.

**中文**: 
RLI（区域锁定推断）是一套重构物理学与人工智能底层的本体论框架。它抛弃了传统统计学习中的连续流形假设，认为宇宙与智能系统的本质是离散的拓扑网络。通过引入**图拉普拉斯谱反馈**实时监测拓扑张力，并触发**A1-坍缩（拓扑坍缩）**机制，RLI 为复杂系统建模、组合优化以及非统计学内生 AI 架构，提供了一种无梯度下降的全新范式。

## Core Concepts / 核心概念

### 1. The A1 Axiom / A1 公理
*   **Definition**: Measurement is the process of establishing a relational lock between two intrinsic structures.
*   **定义**：测量是两个内禀锁定结构之间建立关系锁定的过程。
*   **Ontology**: Regions and relationships precede entities. A system is defined by its internal topological connectivity rather than isolated particles.

### 2. Topological Tension (Pain) / 拓扑张力
*   Traditional GNNs suffer from over-smoothing and over-squashing. RLI monitors the **Fiedler value** (algebraic connectivity) of the graph Laplacian. A rapid decrease in connectivity represents high "Topological Tension", indicating the system is trapped in a shattered solution space (e.g., in NP-hard problems).
